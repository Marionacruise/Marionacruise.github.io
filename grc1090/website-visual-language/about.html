About us

At Tears of Joy, we believe hot sauce should make you happy, even if it makes you cry a little. What started as a weekend hobby in a tiny kitchen, with a blender, a few bags of peppers, and a lot of taste-testing friends, has grown into a family of twenty sauces made for every kind of eater.

We started with a simple idea: everyone deserves a hot sauce that fits them. Some people want a gentle kick, and others want to feel the burn. That’s why we craft our sauces from many different peppers, from smoky chipotles and fruity habaneros to fiery ghost peppers. Each one has its own flavor and personality.

Every bottle is made in small batches with whole peppers, real fruit, and fresh garlic. Nothing artificial, no shortcuts. We roast, ferment, and blend by hand, then taste every batch before it goes into a bottle, so the flavor is always as bold as the heat.

Our heat scale makes it easy to find your match. Start with Mild if you’re new to spice, work your way up through Medium and Hot, and when you’re ready, take on the sauces in our “Why Did I Do This?” collection. However far you go, we’ll be cheering you on.
